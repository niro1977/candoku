How to play

  1. Put one candy in every color.
  2. Only one candy per row and one per column.
  3. Candies can never touch, not even diagonally.

Tap a square once for an X, tap the X to remove it. Double tap a square for a candy. Hold a square, or switch on Maybe, to mark a ? on a square you are unsure about. You start with 3 hints and win 1 more for every 5 levels you pass. A hint shows where an X belongs, and places a candy only when it is the last choice. Every board has exactly one answer.
